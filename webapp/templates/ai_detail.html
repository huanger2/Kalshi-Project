Edge is this AI's predicted win % minus Kalshi's, computed before the game was decided. A skilled, well-calibrated AI should show a positive average edge on games its pick went on to win and a negative one on games it went on to lose -- edge that looks the same either way isn't actually predictive, whatever the Brier score says. "Called the winner" counts a >50%/<50% pick that matched the outcome, regardless of margin; exactly 50% doesn't count as calling either side. NFL ties count as no team winning: they're left out of this and the edge averages (a pick for either side was neither right nor wrong) but count toward the Brier score as a 0.
