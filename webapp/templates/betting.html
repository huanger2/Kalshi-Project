Rules

Edge is the odds you bet on (fair odds, or the raw AI odds) for a side minus what one contract on that side costs (its ask), in points. Each game's better side is bet if its edge clears the minimum; when more games qualify than the daily cap covers, the biggest edges get the money. Changes apply from the next daily run; the What if? numbers follow your edits (and your AI weights) as you type, before you save.
